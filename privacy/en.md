# App Footprint — Privacy policy

Publisher: SioYoo. Support and questions: https://github.com/SioYooo/app-footprint/issues

Updated: 2026-09-23

App Footprint processes diagnostic data on your Mac. We do not receive your diagnostic data, run analytics, or track you.

While monitoring is enabled, the app stores resource counters, process names and identifiers, incidents, diagnosis results, and verification history locally. It does not read command-line arguments, environment variables, or network destinations. You can pause monitoring and choose a history retention period in Settings. Routine samples not tied to incidents follow the selected retention period. Evidence for open incidents is kept. Closed incidents and their frozen evidence are eligible for removal 90 days after closure. Maintenance runs while the app is open.

Storage review scans only folders you choose. File names, paths, and sizes remain in memory and are not added to reports. A local bookmark remembers the folder you approved. Only items you select and confirm are moved to the Trash.

The optional Sensors helper broadcasts temperatures, fan speeds, and process I/O readings within your login session. Other local apps can observe that broadcast. It contains process IDs and name digests, not process names, paths, or command arguments.

Reports stay on your Mac until you choose to export and share them. Redaction is on by default; inspect the preview before sharing. Disk space information is included in diagnostic reports you choose to export for troubleshooting.

Apple processes purchases and restores under its own privacy policy. App Footprint uses verified StoreKit transactions for access and does not receive your payment card or Apple account credentials. There is no App Footprint account.

To stop collection, pause monitoring or quit the app. Local data can be removed from the App Footprint app container in Finder after quitting; deleting the app alone may leave its data behind. Files you exported are separate and must be removed separately.
